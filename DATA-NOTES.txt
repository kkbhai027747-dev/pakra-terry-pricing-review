DATA GUIDE — historical records, not a fresh store audit

data/historical-pricing-samples-10.csv / .json
Ten actual saved examples across low, mid and high source amounts, with Chinese,
Japanese and English card versions represented. JSON retains the full provenance;
CSV is convenient for a spreadsheet. Source prices are CNY, Shopify prices USD.

These are arithmetic/model-review examples, not confirmed wrong-price cases.
All ten recompute to the saved Shopify price under the current 40% formula. That
does not verify their market value, selected condition or today's source price.
For example, a CNY 0.50 card becomes USD 25 because the listing includes CNY 85
of shipping allowances; a CNY 5 card becomes USD 27. Terry can use these to examine
the shipping/model concern without mistaking it for a calculation error.

data/historical-shopify-mapping-120.csv / .json
120 historical exact identity/product/variant mappings. These are review records,
not enabled mapping configuration. The included IDs are unique. Saved prices and
availability were checked against locally saved Shopify readbacks. All recorded
products are in display-only mode; current availability was not checked.

data/provenance.json
Source paths and newly computed SHA-256 checksums for the 117 local evidence
inputs used to build these exports. Supplied historical hashes were also checked
where present. Paths are relative to Jun's original repository and are not links
to files bundled here. Raw app screenshots, capture UI dumps and full historical
execution folders are omitted. They remain on Jun's side for a targeted follow-up.

Key fields
  identity: pokemon:<language>:<set_id>:<source_uuid>; do not match by name alone.
  source_uuid: exact Jihuanshe card/version identifier from saved evidence.
  set_id / set_code / card_number: source identity context, not Shopify IDs.
  product_id / variant_id / sku: exact saved Shopify correspondence.
  historical_source_asking_price_cny: saved source amount, not a current quote.
  source_quote_observed_at_utc: actual recorded source observation time.
  source_condition_scope: app_unfiltered_version_minimum, not NM or another
    verified condition; condition_filtered is false for every example.
  historical_shopify_price_usd: price in the selected saved readback.
  historical_readback_or_completion_at_utc: timestamp and basis are separate;
    a completion timestamp is not relabeled as a raw readback capture time.
    Null means the readback omitted its own timestamp. Use the audit time as an
    explicitly weaker supporting timestamp, not an invented observation time.
  supporting_audit_at_utc: time of the saved audit supporting the row.
  recomputed_price_usd / calculation: reproduction of the existing formula.
  verified_wrong_price_example: false; no confirmed market-price error asserted.
  automation_enabled_by_this_handoff: false.

Times are UTC ISO timestamps unless explicitly converted. File modification dates
are not used as capture dates because files were copied during workstation migration.
Every example is too old for an operational same-day/four-hour quotation check.

Observed run status is historical. No device, background worker or scheduler was
queried or started for this handoff, so current runtime/schedule status is unknown.
